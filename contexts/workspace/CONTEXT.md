# Workspace

The top-level space where work happens. Everything else (projects, agent profiles) belongs to a workspace.

## Language

**Workspace**:
The top-level container of work; only its Members can see or change it.
_Avoid_: Organization, portfolio, team

**Member**:
An Account that has access to a Workspace. The Account that creates a Workspace becomes its first Member.
_Avoid_: Owner, user, participant

**Project**:
A unit of work inside a Workspace.
